Plumbing Marketing in Texas: How to Get 10+ New Customers Every Month

The 2026 playbook for Texas plumbing companies that want predictable revenue instead of feast-or-famine cycles.

Citadel Solutions · February 2026 · 7 min read

Texas has over 58,000 active plumbing licenses. In metro areas like Houston, DFW, San Antonio, and Austin, the competition for plumbing jobs is brutal. If your phone isn't ringing consistently, the problem isn't your work — it's your marketing.

Most plumbing companies rely on three things: referrals, Yelp, and hope. That works when you're a one-truck operation. But the moment you add a second crew, hire an apprentice, or try to grow past $500K in revenue, you need a system.

Here are 6 strategies Texas plumbing companies are using to book 10+ new customers every month — with real cost numbers.

The Revenue Problem Most Plumbers Face

Quick math on why marketing matters:

  • Average residential plumbing job in Texas: $350-800
  • Average commercial plumbing contract: $2,000-15,000
  • If you're closing 20 residential jobs/month at $500 average = $10K/mo revenue
  • Switch just 5 of those to commercial at $5K average = $32,500/mo

The highest-growth plumbing companies in Texas aren't chasing more residential drain cleanings. They're going after commercial contracts — property managers, restaurant owners, HOAs, and general contractors who need reliable plumbing service on retainer.

The question is: how do you find those accounts?

1. Proactive Email Outreach to Commercial Accounts

This is the fastest way to get in front of commercial decision-makers who have budget and ongoing plumbing needs. Instead of waiting for them to search Google, you reach out directly with a targeted email sequence.

How it works:

  • Build a list of property managers, HOAs, restaurant groups, and GCs in your service area
  • Send a 4-email sequence over 2-3 weeks — introduce your company, highlight experience, ask for a quick call
  • Interested prospects reply directly to you — no middleman, no shared leads
  • Average response rate: 3-8% (from a list of 500, that's 15-40 conversations)

Why this works in Texas: Texas metros are booming with commercial construction and property management. Houston alone has 12,000+ commercial properties. DFW has even more. These property managers need plumbers on speed dial — and most are currently unhappy with their existing vendor. The first plumber to reach out proactively wins.

Cost breakdown:

  • DIY route: $50-100/mo for email tools — but requires 5-10 hrs/week of your time
  • Done-for-you: $1,500/mo flat — hands off, you just take the calls
  • Expected ROI: 8-15 qualified estimate requests per month. At $5K avg commercial job, that's $40K-75K in pipeline from a $1,500 investment.

2. Google Business Profile Optimization (Free)

If you're not showing up in Google's Map Pack for "plumber near me" searches, you're losing 40%+ of potential residential customers to competitors who are.

Texas-specific GBP strategy:

  • List every city and suburb in your service area. Texas metros are sprawling — DFW alone covers 40+ cities. If you serve Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Allen — list all of them.
  • Add service categories aggressively. "Plumber" is obvious. Also add: drain cleaning, water heater repair, sewer repair, gas line installation, backflow testing.
  • Post weekly. Photos of completed jobs, seasonal tips ("Texas freeze coming — here's how to protect your pipes"), quick maintenance tips.
  • Chase reviews like they're revenue — because they are. 100+ reviews with a 4.7+ rating dominates the Map Pack in most Texas markets.

3. Google Local Services Ads ($25-50/lead)

LSAs are the top-of-page ad unit that shows your star rating, reviews, and "Google Guaranteed" badge. For plumbing, these are the highest-quality inbound leads available.

Texas plumbing LSA benchmarks:

  • Cost per lead: $25-50 (lower than HVAC due to faster job turnaround)
  • Lead quality: High intent — caller has an active plumbing issue
  • Close rate: 30-50% for responsive companies
  • Typical monthly budget: $1,000-3,000

Pro tip: Response speed is everything with LSAs. The plumbing company that calls back within 5 minutes wins the job 80% of the time. Set up call forwarding to make sure every lead gets answered live.

4. Referral Program With Real Incentives

Texas operates on relationships. The plumbing companies growing fastest aren't just doing good work — they're systematically asking for referrals and making it worth people's time.

What a real referral program looks like:

  • $50 Visa gift card for any referral that turns into a booked job
  • $200 for commercial referrals — a property manager intro is worth 10x a residential referral
  • Text message follow-up 48 hours after every completed job: "Thanks for choosing us! Know anyone who needs plumbing work? We'll send you a $50 gift card for any referral."
  • Quarterly reminder to past customers via email or text blast

Texas reality: In tight-knit Texas communities — especially suburbs like Katy, Southlake, Gilbert — one referral from the right person (an HOA board member, a real estate agent, a property manager) can generate 10-20 jobs over the next year. Invest in the relationship.

5. Strategic Partnerships With Complementary Trades

Every HVAC company, electrician, and general contractor in your area has customers who also need plumbing work. And you have customers who need their services. This is the most underutilized growth channel in Texas home services.

High-value partnerships for plumbing companies:

  • HVAC companies — cross-refer for shared projects (new construction, remodels)
  • Real estate agents — become their go-to plumber for inspections and repairs before closing
  • Property management companies — become their preferred plumbing vendor
  • Home warranty companies — some Texas home warranty providers are actively recruiting plumbing contractors
  • Restoration companies — water damage = plumbing work

Build just 3 strong partnerships and you could add 10-15 jobs per month with zero marketing spend.

6. SEO — The Long Game ($500-1,500/mo)

SEO takes 4-6 months to start working, but once it does, you get leads essentially for free. The key for Texas plumbing companies: go hyper-local.

Page strategy:

  • Create a unique page for every service: "drain cleaning [city]", "water heater repair [city]", "sewer line replacement [city]"
  • Create a unique page for every major suburb you serve
  • Write 2 blog posts per month answering real customer questions ("How much does a slab leak repair cost in Houston?" "When to replace vs repair a water heater in Texas")

The plumbing companies winning SEO in Texas markets have 50-100+ pages on their site. Most competitors have 5-10. More pages = more keywords = more traffic = more leads.

The Bottom Line: Pick 3 and Execute

You don't need all 6 channels running simultaneously. Here's the recommended priority:

  1. Google Business Profile — free, start today
  2. Referral program — systematize what's already working
  3. Commercial email outreach — fastest path to high-value contracts
  4. LSAs — high-quality residential leads
  5. Partnerships — build 2-3 this quarter
  6. SEO — start now, results in 6 months

The plumbing companies that will dominate Texas in 2026 aren't the biggest or the cheapest. They're the ones with a system for generating leads predictably, so their crews stay busy and their revenue stays stable.
