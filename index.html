FAQ

Straight Answers to the Questions You're Thinking.

Is cold email even legal?

Yes. 100%. The CAN-SPAM Act allows commercial email as long as it includes a real physical address, an unsubscribe option, and isn't deceptive. Every email we send is fully compliant. We also use dedicated domains and proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so nothing touches your main business email.

How is this different from buying leads on Angi or HomeAdvisor?

Those platforms sell the same lead to 5–10 companies. You're competing on speed — whoever calls first wins. Our leads are exclusive to you. We reach out on your behalf, the prospect replies to you, and nobody else is in the conversation. No bidding wars. No shared leads. Ever.

How long until I see results?

Most campaigns start generating replies within 7–10 days. We spend the first week building your list and warming up sending domains. By week two, emails are going out. By the end of month one, you should have booked meetings on your calendar.

What if I'm already doing Google Ads or SEO?

Keep it running. Cold email doesn't compete with your other marketing — it adds a completely new channel. Ads are inbound — you wait for people to search. Email is outbound — we go find them. The combination is what fills your calendar consistently.

What do I actually have to do?

Show up to the meetings we book. That's it. We handle list building, email writing, sending, follow-ups, and scheduling. You might spend 15 minutes a week reviewing results with us. The rest is on us.

Why only 5 clients per market?

Because if we put two roofing companies in the same metro hitting the same prospect list, we'd be working against ourselves — and against you. Market exclusivity means we protect your territory. First come, first served.

What if I'm already getting enough work?

Great — but is it the right work? Cold email lets you target specific job types, commercial vs. residential, and geographic areas. Stop taking every $500 service call and start filling your pipeline with $10,000+ projects.

Will this hurt my reputation? Is it spam?

Our emails are personalized, relevant, and sent from properly authenticated domains with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. They look and feel like a real business professional reaching out — because that's exactly what they are. We maintain strict CAN-SPAM compliance and never exceed industry-standard sending volumes.

What's the contract?

Month-to-month. No long-term commitment. No setup fees. No cancellation penalties. We keep clients because we deliver results, not because they're locked into a contract.

What if nobody replies?

In 3+ years of B2B cold email operations, a properly targeted campaign to home services businesses has never produced zero results. If your campaign underperforms our benchmarks, we adjust targeting, messaging, and volume at no extra cost. Our reputation depends on your success.
