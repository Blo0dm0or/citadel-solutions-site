FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

How is this different from Google Ads or HomeAdvisor?

Those platforms charge you per click or per lead — and you're competing with every other contractor in your area for the same eyeballs. We go direct. We identify specific decision-makers (property managers, GCs, facility managers) and reach out to them personally on your behalf. No bidding wars. No shared leads.

Is cold email legal?

Yes. B2B cold email is fully legal under CAN-SPAM regulations when done correctly — with a real business address, honest subject lines, and a clear opt-out. We're fully compliant on every send.

How long before I see results?

Most clients start seeing qualified replies within 2-3 weeks of campaign launch. Booked meetings typically start within 30-45 days. Full pipeline momentum by 60 days.

Do I have to write the emails?

No. We write everything — subject lines, email body, follow-up sequences. All personalized to your business, your market, and your services. You approve them before anything goes out.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime. No contracts, no cancellation fees, no guilt trips. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking you in.

Why is this so affordable compared to other agencies?

We use advanced technology and lean operations to deliver premium results without the premium overhead. No bloated team, no office, no account managers billing you $150/hr. You're paying for results, not headcount.
